Neil Armstrong autograph: how to tell real from fake

He stopped signing in 1994. Autopen and forgeries are everywhere.

For years Neil Armstrong answered autograph requests by post, most often on the official NASA portrait of him in a white spacesuit. In 1994 he stopped signing altogether, which fixed the supply and pushed prices up.

His is now one of the most forged signatures in any field, and NASA's use of Autopen machines adds a second layer of difficulty.

What a genuine Neil Armstrong signature looks like

  • A fluid signature in which the capital N and A are the clearest letters, with the remaining letters running together.
  • Many genuine examples are inscribed to a named person, because that is how he usually answered mail requests.
  • The white spacesuit NASA lithograph is the classic signed item.

Common fakes and red flags

  • Autopen signatures. The machine reproduces a stored pattern, so two autopen signatures overlay each other exactly, the line has even pressure throughout, and there are often small dots where the pen started and stopped.
  • Printed signatures on NASA lithographs.
  • Forgeries on uninscribed photos. Because uninscribed examples are worth more, they are what forgers produce.
  • Items said to have been signed after 1994.

How to verify a Neil Armstrong autograph

  • Compare the signature with known Armstrong autopen patterns; a perfect match means machine-signed.
  • Check the line under magnification for the varying pressure of a hand-held pen.
  • Use an authenticator with space memorabilia expertise. Astronaut autographs are a specialist area.

Neil Armstrong autograph FAQ

Did Neil Armstrong sign autographs?

Yes, for many years, mainly in response to requests by post. He stopped in 1994 and declined requests after that.

How can I tell if an Armstrong signature is autopen?

An autopen signature is identical to others from the same pattern, has a uniform line with no pressure changes, and often shows slight shakiness or dots at the start and end of strokes.

Is an inscribed Neil Armstrong photo worth less?

Inscribed examples usually sell for less than uninscribed ones, but an inscription is also reassuring, because it matches how he normally signed.
