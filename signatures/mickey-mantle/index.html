Mickey Mantle autograph: how to tell real from fake

Two very different signatures: the 1950s rookie hand and the famous card-show autograph.

Mickey Mantle signed a great deal, especially at card shows and private signings in the 1980s and early 1990s, so authentic material is available. Demand is so strong that forgeries are just as plentiful.

Mantle's signature changed more than most. Knowing which era an item claims to be from tells you which signature it should carry.

What a genuine Mickey Mantle signature looks like

  • The later signature, seen on most show-signed items, is large, rounded and very consistent, with two prominent half-moon shaped capital Ms.
  • Early 1950s signatures are smaller and plainer, without the sweeping M. A rookie-era item with the late show signature does not add up.
  • Later signatures are evenly spaced and carefully placed: on the sweet spot of a ball, or in a clear area of a photo.
  • Mantle signed for Upper Deck Authenticated in the 1990s, and those items carry a numbered UDA hologram.

Common fakes and red flags

  • Clubhouse signatures on 1950s and 1960s Yankees team balls, where a clubhouse attendant signed for star players.
  • Forgeries of the late show signature. It is so regular that it is easy to imitate at a glance; look for hesitation and uneven pressure.
  • Period mismatches, such as a late-style signature on a 1950s photo described as "signed at the time".

How to verify a Mickey Mantle autograph

  • Match the signature style to the claimed date of signing.
  • For UDA items, confirm the hologram number with Upper Deck.
  • For everything else, look for PSA/DNA, JSA or Beckett certification and verify the certificate number on the authenticator's own website.

Mickey Mantle autograph FAQ

Why do Mickey Mantle signatures look so different?

His handwriting evolved. The compact signature of the early 1950s gradually became the large, rounded signature with half-moon M's that he used at shows later in life.

Are Mickey Mantle signed team baseballs reliable?

Treat them with care. On vintage Yankees team balls some star signatures were added by clubhouse staff, so each signature on the ball needs to be assessed individually.

What authentication should a Mantle autograph have?

Either original Upper Deck Authenticated paperwork with a matching hologram, or certification from PSA/DNA, JSA or Beckett.
