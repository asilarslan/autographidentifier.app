Kobe Bryant autograph: how to tell real from fake

Upper Deck first, Panini from 2009, and a market flooded with fakes after 2020.

Kobe Bryant signed under exclusive memorabilia deals for most of his career: first with Upper Deck Authenticated, then with Panini Authentic from 2009. Items from those programmes are the benchmark for authentic Kobe autographs.

After his death in January 2020 prices rose sharply, and the number of forged jerseys, basketballs and photos on resale sites rose with them.

What a genuine Kobe Bryant signature looks like

  • Early-career signatures are fuller, with more of "Kobe Bryant" written out.
  • Later signatures are faster and more abbreviated, built around a large K and B with the remaining letters reduced to a few strokes.
  • He often added his number, 8 or 24, or a short inscription. The number should match the era of the item.

Common fakes and red flags

  • A "24" inscription on an item from the years when he wore 8, or the reverse.
  • Jerseys and balls with only a generic certificate from the seller.
  • Counterfeit holograms. A sticker is only meaningful if its number checks out with the company that issued it.
  • Reprint photos with a printed signature offered as hand-signed.

How to verify a Kobe Bryant autograph

  • For Upper Deck or Panini items, verify the hologram or certificate number directly with the issuing company.
  • Otherwise ask for PSA/DNA, JSA or Beckett certification and confirm the number on their website.
  • Compare against certified examples from the same seasons, not just any Kobe signature.

Kobe Bryant autograph FAQ

Which company authenticated Kobe Bryant autographs?

Upper Deck Authenticated handled his exclusive signed memorabilia early in his career, and Panini Authentic took over in 2009. Third-party authenticators such as PSA/DNA, JSA and Beckett also certify his signature.

Why are there so many fake Kobe autographs?

Demand and prices jumped after 2020 while the supply of genuine items is fixed, which makes forgeries profitable. Most problem items are sold without verifiable certification.

Should a Kobe autograph include 8 or 24?

Not necessarily, but if a number is inscribed it should agree with the era of the item: 8 for the earlier seasons, 24 from 2006-07 onward.
