The Beatles autograph: how to tell real from fake

Full sets are rare. Many were signed by road managers, not by the band.

A full set of all four Beatles signatures is one of the most desirable autograph items in music, and one of the most problematic. A large share of the sets in circulation were not signed by John, Paul, George and Ringo.

That includes outright forgeries, but also a great many period signatures written by people who worked for the band.

What a genuine The Beatles signature looks like

  • Genuine sets mostly date from 1962 to 1964, when the band still signed for fans in person. As Beatlemania grew, complete sets became much harder to obtain.
  • Each member's signature changed noticeably during the 1960s, so all four signatures on one item should be consistent with the same date.
  • On a real set the four signatures differ in pressure, slant and rhythm, because four people wrote them. They are often in the same pen but not in the same hand.

Common fakes and red flags

  • Signatures by road managers Neil Aspinall and Mal Evans, who regularly signed for the band. These are period items and fool many buyers.
  • Fan club and secretarial signatures sent out by post.
  • Printed signatures on promotional cards and fan club photos.
  • Four signatures that share the same slant and letter shapes, which points to a single writer.

How to verify a The Beatles autograph

  • Establish the date and place of signing, and check it against where the band actually was.
  • Beatles autographs are a specialist field. Major auction houses rely on dedicated Beatles experts in addition to the general authentication companies.
  • Be very cautious with full sets that have no documented history.

The Beatles autograph FAQ

Are most Beatles autographs fake?

A very large proportion of full sets on the market are not genuine. Many are period signatures by road managers or fan club staff rather than modern forgeries, but they are still not signed by the band.

Who signed for The Beatles?

Road managers Neil Aspinall and Mal Evans are the best known. They signed all four names on photos, programmes and album covers when the band could not keep up with requests.

How much is a full set of Beatles autographs worth?

Authenticated full sets are among the most expensive music autographs, with the price depending heavily on the item signed and its provenance. An unauthenticated set has little market value.
