Michael Jordan autograph: how to tell real from fake

Upper Deck exclusive since 1992 and one of the most forged names in sports.

Michael Jordan is one of the most valuable and most forged autographs in modern sports. He has rarely signed in public for decades, which means the supply of loose, uncertified "in-person" Jordan signatures is far smaller than the number offered for sale.

Since 1992 Jordan has had an exclusive autograph agreement with Upper Deck. Items signed under that deal are sold as Upper Deck Authenticated (UDA) and carry a serial-numbered hologram that can be looked up on Upper Deck's website.

What a genuine Michael Jordan signature looks like

  • A large, looping capital M that dominates the first name; the rest of "Michael" is usually compressed into a few fast strokes.
  • A tall J with a long descending loop, followed by a quick, flowing "ordan" that often trails upward.
  • Signed at speed in one confident motion. Genuine examples look fluid; the line does not wobble or stop mid-letter.
  • 1980s signatures are fuller and more legible than the streamlined signature seen on later UDA items, so compare against examples from the same period.

Common fakes and red flags

  • Unhologrammed jerseys, basketballs and photos with only a seller-printed certificate.
  • Facsimile (pre-printed) signatures on cards, posters and mass-produced photos that are sold as hand-signed.
  • Slow, carefully drawn signatures that copy the shape but lack natural speed.
  • UDA holograms that do not return a matching item description when checked with Upper Deck.

How to verify a Michael Jordan autograph

  • If the item has a UDA hologram, look the serial number up with Upper Deck and confirm the description matches the item in your hands.
  • For items without UDA paperwork, a third-party opinion from PSA/DNA, JSA or Beckett is the hobby standard before paying serious money.
  • Ask the seller where and when it was signed. Vague provenance on a high-value Jordan item is a warning sign.

Michael Jordan autograph FAQ

How much is a Michael Jordan autograph worth?

It depends on the item, the era of the signature and above all the authentication. Certified items with UDA, PSA/DNA, JSA or Beckett paperwork sell for many times the price of uncertified ones, because buyers cannot otherwise rule out a forgery.

Does Michael Jordan still sign autographs?

He signs through his exclusive arrangement with Upper Deck rather than at public signings, which is why Upper Deck Authenticated items are the main source of modern Jordan autographs.

Can an app tell me if my Jordan autograph is real?

An AI scan can give you a quick first opinion and point out red flags, such as a signature that looks printed or drawn. It is not a certificate. For anything valuable, follow up with an established third-party authenticator.
