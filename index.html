Frequently asked questions

Is Autograph Identifier free?

The app is free to download and includes 1 free scan so you can see a full result before paying anything. Premium unlocks unlimited scans for $9.99 per week or $49.99 per year.

What does a scan tell me?

Each result gives the name the signature appears to belong to, an overall assessment such as Likely Authentic or Unclear, the signature characteristics the AI noticed, any red flags and a confidence level.

Is the result a certificate of authenticity?

No. It is an AI-generated first opinion that helps you sort items and decide what deserves a closer look. For valuable pieces you should still use a professional authenticator such as PSA/DNA, JSA or Beckett.

What can I scan?

Signed photos, baseballs, jerseys, cards, album covers, books and documents. A sharp, well-lit, close photo of the signature gives the best result. Clear, well-known signatures are identified most reliably; faint or heavily stylised ones may come back as Unclear.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17.5 or later, and Android phones and tablets through Google Play.

How do I cancel Premium?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. Cancel any time from your App Store or Google Play subscription settings and you keep access until the end of the paid period.
